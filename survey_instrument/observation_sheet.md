## Agency Code — Quick Reference

| Code | Label | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Passive receipt | Copies and submits. No pause, no re-reading, no changes. |
| **2** | Surface engagement | Pauses, re-reads, minor changes. No questioning of content or meaning. |
| **3** | Active interrogation | Asks "is this right?", checks another source, rewrites in own words, spots an error. |

## Follow-up Questions — Ask after task is collected

**Q1:** *"Is the answer you wrote yours, or did you get it from somewhere?"*

| Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|---|---|
| "From somewhere" / points at phone | "Sort of" / hesitates | "Mine" / "I worked it out" |

**Q2:** *"Could you explain your answer to me right now if I asked you to?"*

| Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|---|---|
| "No" / silence | Starts, trails off | Clear explanation in own words |

**For non-verbal students:** Show the printed card from `student_questions_card.md`. Accept pointing.
**If student appears anxious:** Skip Q2. Write "skipped" in Notes. Wellbeing always comes first.
