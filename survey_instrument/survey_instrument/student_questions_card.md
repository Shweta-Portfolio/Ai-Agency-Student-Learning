# Student Questions Card
## Print and laminate. Show to student after task. They just point — no writing or reading required.

---

## Question 1

# Where did your answer come from?

## 👤 It's mine — I worked it out myself

## 🤝 Sort of — I used some help

## 📱 From somewhere — phone / screen / friend

---

## Question 2

# Could you explain your answer right now?

## ✅ Yes — I could explain it

## 🤔 Sort of — I could explain some of it

## ❌ No — I'm not sure I could

---

*Just point to the one that feels right. There are no wrong answers.*
